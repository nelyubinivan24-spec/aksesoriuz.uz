# Offer Strategy

## Purpose

Define the commercial offers and calls to action that will drive conversion across the website.

## Primary Offer

Custom branded products and accessories for restaurants, hotels, and businesses.

## Hero Offer Direction

Recommended homepage hero:

Produce branded details for your business: menu covers, packaging, corporate gifts, uniforms, and custom accessories made to match your brand.

Support points:

- custom production;
- multiple materials;
- branding technologies;
- business-focused categories;
- consultation and quote request.

## Core Offers

### Offer 1: Request a Quote

Use when:

- user knows what they need;
- product quantity and branding matter;
- price depends on materials and customization.

Fields:

- name;
- phone;
- Telegram or WhatsApp;
- category;
- approximate quantity;
- message;
- file/logo upload later if technically supported.

CTA labels:

- Request a quote
- Get calculation
- Calculate order

### Offer 2: Order a Sample

Use when:

- quality and materials are the main concern;
- buyer wants confidence before larger order.

CTA labels:

- Order a sample
- Request sample
- See materials

### Offer 3: Send a Brief

Use when:

- custom production is involved;
- dimensions, materials, or references are needed.

CTA labels:

- Send a brief
- Discuss custom order
- Start custom project

### Offer 4: Fast Contact

Use when:

- mobile users want immediate communication;
- procurement users need quick confirmation.

Channels:

- phone;
- Telegram;
- WhatsApp;
- email.

CTA labels:

- Call now
- Write on Telegram
- Write on WhatsApp

## Page-Level CTA Strategy

Homepage:

- primary: Request a quote;
- secondary: View catalog;
- tertiary: Order a sample.

Catalog:

- primary: Request calculation;
- secondary: Filter by industry;
- tertiary: Contact manager.

Product page:

- primary: Request this product;
- secondary: Order sample;
- tertiary: Ask about materials.

Service page:

- primary: Discuss project;
- secondary: View related products;
- tertiary: Ask for technology advice.

Industry page:

- primary: Get a set for your business;
- secondary: View products;
- tertiary: Send venue requirements.

Contacts:

- primary: Submit inquiry;
- secondary: phone/Telegram/WhatsApp.

## Conversion Friction To Remove

- Do not force users to know internal product codes.
- Do not make users search through all products before asking.
- Do not hide contact actions in the footer.
- Do not use generic "Buy" buttons if pricing is quote-based.
- Do not show placeholder/demo products.

## Trust Builders

Recommended trust signals:

- years of experience;
- number of clients;
- production technologies;
- material range;
- client logos;
- real product gallery;
- delivery geography;
- minimum order guidance;
- response time promise.

## Pricing Strategy

Approved direction:

The primary conversion path should lead to project discussion or quote request, not direct self-service purchase.

Recommended implementation:

- explain what affects price;
- offer quote request;
- provide minimum order guidance.
- show "from" prices only if accurate and maintainable;
- optionally provide a downloadable or viewable price catalog.

## Price Catalog Option

The website should be ready to support a price catalog if the business decides to publish one.

Possible formats:

- downloadable PDF catalog;
- online catalog page with price ranges;
- gated download after contact submission;
- manager-sent catalog after Telegram/WhatsApp request.

Recommended default:

Start quote-first. Add a price catalog only when pricing is accurate, maintained, and aligned with sales strategy.

UX note:

If a price catalog exists, it should support conversion rather than replace consultation. Recommended CTA pairing:

- View price catalog;
- Request exact calculation;
- Send quantity and logo for final price.

## Open Questions

- What is the minimum order per product type?
- Is sample ordering paid or free?
- What is the realistic response time?
- Which channel is preferred: phone, Telegram, WhatsApp, or form?
- Should the price catalog be public, downloadable, or available after request?
- Which product categories can safely show price ranges?
