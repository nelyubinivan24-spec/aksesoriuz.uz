## Current Rebuild Coverage

Implemented in the new project before Stage 13:

- RU and EN core routes;
- 6 top-level catalog groups;
- 10 production/service capability pages;
- 41 legacy category/subcategory mappings in `src/data/catalog.ts`;
- 32 B2B reference product cards in `src/data/products.ts`;
- product detail routes for RU and EN;
- B2B project brief CTAs instead of self-service purchase mechanics.

## Coverage Gaps

The current rebuilt site is structurally aligned, but not yet product-complete.

Important gaps:

- only 32 of 255 original product URLs are represented as product/reference cards;
- missing product/reference coverage for `PDSTKN`, `VZTFL`, `KYCHN`, and `EBJ` families;
- category pages do not yet expose dedicated subcategory landing pages;
- old product URLs are not yet mapped one-by-one into a migration manifest;
- final product text, images, materials, dimensions, MOQ, and timing still require client data or controlled extraction from the current site;
- legacy SEO redirects are not defined yet.

## Stage 13 Recommendation

Next stage should be: Complete B2B catalog coverage system.

Required outcomes:

1. Add a legacy migration manifest for all 255 product URLs.
2. Add canonical product-family IDs and map every original SKU family to one of them.
3. Add subcategory landing-page data for every original category/subcategory.
4. Decide whether each original SKU becomes:
   - a full product page;
   - a lightweight reference page;
   - a redirect into a family/subcategory page;
   - a hidden migration record until final cards are approved.
5. Keep project-brief conversion on every product/family page.
6. Prepare redirect and SEO rules for old URLs before launch.

## Acceptance Criteria For Future Stages

The rebuild can move forward only if:

- every original product group has a new destination;
- every original service/capability has a new destination;
- every original product URL has a migration decision;
- no page introduces self-service purchase or payment language;
- product cards remain B2B references until final commercial data is approved.
