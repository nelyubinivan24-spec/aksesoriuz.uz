# Market Research Plan

## Purpose

Audience and category emphasis should be guided by market research, not only by the existing website structure.

The new website should stay universal, but homepage hierarchy, SEO pages, and campaign priorities should reflect demand, profitability, and conversion potential.

## Research Goals

1. Identify high-demand B2B product categories.
2. Identify high-value audience segments.
3. Understand competitor positioning.
4. Identify SEO opportunities.
5. Find gaps in local and regional websites that Aksessoriuz can outperform.

## Research Scope

Markets:

- Uzbekistan;
- Tashkent;
- neighboring regional markets where relevant.

Segments:

- restaurants and cafes;
- hotels and hospitality;
- corporate gifts and business products;
- packaging and souvenirs;
- uniforms and textile products;
- production services such as UV printing, laser engraving, embossing, foiling, and printing.

## Research Questions

- Which products are searched for most often by B2B buyers?
- Which segments have urgent buying triggers such as openings, rebrands, events, or seasonal campaigns?
- Which competitors rank for commercial searches?
- How do competitors present pricing?
- How do competitors collect leads?
- Which categories are poorly served online?
- Which categories need separate SEO landing pages?
- What proof points do competitors lack?

## Sources To Use

- search results for commercial keywords;
- competitor websites;
- marketplace/category signals where relevant;
- SEO keyword tools if available;
- client sales data when provided;
- internal knowledge about margins, order size, and repeat purchases.

## Output

Create `docs/MARKET_RESEARCH.md` with:

- key findings;
- competitor map;
- demand signals;
- SEO keyword groups;
- recommended audience emphasis;
- recommended catalog hierarchy;
- implications for homepage and conversion paths.

## Current Strategic Rule

Until research and profitability data are available, design for broad B2B acquisition while making the structure flexible enough to promote the strongest categories later.
