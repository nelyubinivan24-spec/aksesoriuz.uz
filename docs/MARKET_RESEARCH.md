# Market Research

## Status

Initial qualitative research draft.

This document is based on public search and website review performed on 2026-05-31. It is not a numeric keyword-volume study yet. It should guide Stage 1 strategy and Stage 2 conversion architecture, then be refined when sales data, margins, product lists, and keyword tools are available.

## Executive Summary

The market supports a universal B2B website strategy. Aksessoriuz should not present itself as a narrow restaurant accessory producer or a generic souvenir supplier. The strongest opportunity is to position the company as a custom branded production partner across five commercial demand clusters:

1. HoReCa and hospitality accessories.
2. Corporate gifts and business products.
3. Premium packaging and branded presentation.
4. Uniforms and textile products.
5. Production technologies: UV printing, laser cutting/engraving, embossing, foiling, printing, and custom fabrication.

The competitive landscape is fragmented. Many competitors are strong in one vertical, but few combine product catalog, production technologies, industry pages, client proof, quote mechanics, and premium B2B storytelling in one coherent experience.

Recommended strategic direction:

- Keep the site universal.
- Make homepage navigation split by business type, product category, and production technology.
- Use quote-first conversion as the main path.
- Add a price catalog option later, but avoid fixed e-commerce mechanics unless pricing is maintained.
- Build SEO landing pages for the strongest clusters.
- Use real work, client logos, materials, and production process as the main trust layer.

## Market Signals

### Uzbekistan B2B Base

Official statistics show a broad B2B market:

- As of March 1, 2026, Uzbekistan had 486,448 operating enterprises and organizations, and 415,066 operating small businesses.
- As of April 1, 2026, there were 1,208,341 small business entities, including 507,138 small enterprises and micro-firms and 701,203 individual entrepreneurs and related entities.

Implication:

The website should support broad B2B acquisition, not only one niche. Many small and medium businesses need branded materials, packaging, uniforms, printed products, and presentation items.

### HoReCA and Hospitality

Official statistics show strong accommodation and food-service activity:

- In 2025, accommodation and food services in Uzbekistan reached about 227 trillion soums.
- This was a 10.4 percent increase compared with 2024.
- Tashkent city accounted for 38.7 trillion soums.

Implication:

Restaurants, cafes, hotels, and hospitality businesses should remain one of the top homepage paths. This segment has recurring needs: menu covers, check holders, reserves, placemats, napkin holders, toothpick packaging, uniforms, tableware, packaging, and branded consumables.

Source:

- National Statistics Committee: `https://stat.uz/en/press-center/news-of-committee/67017-2025-jilda-yashash-va-ov-atlanish-khizmatlari-azhmi-227-trln-s-mga-etdi-4`

### Trade, Retail, and Packaging Demand

Official statistics show large retail and wholesale trade volume:

- In 2025, retail trade turnover reached 482.4 trillion soums, up 11.2 percent.
- Wholesale trade turnover reached 543.3 trillion soums, up 12.5 percent.
- As of January 1, 2026, there were 86,180 commercial enterprises in retail trade and 51,667 in wholesale trade.

Implication:

Packaging, branded bags, premium boxes, product presentation, and gift sets deserve strong placement. Retail and wholesale businesses create demand for brand-visible packaging and printed materials.

## Competitor Map

### Ubrand

URL:

- `https://ubrand.uz/`

Position:

- Branded packaging and consumables for cafes, restaurants, and delivery businesses.

Observed strengths:

- Clear HoReCa focus.
- Uses prices, quantities, and timing on several product entries.
- Explains branded consumables as advertising tools.
- Mentions own production, fast timing, minimum runs, and Uzbekistan-wide work.

Observed opportunity for Aksessoriuz:

- Aksessoriuz can compete by offering a broader product ecosystem: not only consumables, but also durable accessories, premium folders, menu covers, uniforms, packaging, and production services.
- If price catalog is added, it should be cleaner and more structured than a simple list.

### Koja.uz

URL:

- `https://koja.uz/`

Position:

- Leather and faux-leather products, menu folders, document folders, diaries, restaurant and hotel accessories.

Observed strengths:

- Strong category relevance to Aksessoriuz.
- Long history claim.
- Clear product category list.
- Mentions restaurant, bar, hotel, and cafe products.
- Shows client proof section.

Observed weaknesses/opportunities:

- Some UX/copy signals feel template-like or inconsistent.
- The new Aksessoriuz site can outperform through better structure, stronger mobile UX, better product pages, and clearer quote flow.

### Promogift

URL:

- `https://promogift.uz/`

Position:

- Branded promotional gifts and souvenir products.

Observed strengths:

- Direct headline for souvenirs with logo in Tashkent.

- Broad product matrix.
- Telegram/contact CTA appears early.

Observed opportunity for Aksessoriuz:

- Combine technology explanations with richer product catalog and stronger industry-based navigation.
- Avoid becoming only a souvenir site; keep premium production partner positioning.

### VASAKA

URL:

- `https://vasaka.uz/`

Position:

- Premium B2B packaging and branded presentation solutions.

Observed strengths:

- Strong premium B2B positioning.
- Clear focus on custom work.
- Strong trust layer: client logos, production, materials, technologies.
- Quote-first model with individual calculation.
- Clear project accompaniment message.

Observed opportunity for Aksessoriuz:

- Aksessoriuz should adopt the level of clarity and confidence, but keep a broader category scope and more direct catalog utility.

### Web2Print

URL:

- `https://web2print.uz/`

Position:

- Online printing, souvenir, textile, and packaging order platform.

Observed strengths:

- Large catalog.
- Many product pages show prices.
- Online ordering mechanics and visible categories.
- Strong SEO coverage across many printed and branded products.

Observed opportunity for Aksessoriuz:

- Aksessoriuz should not copy pure e-commerce if prices are variable.
- Compete with expertise, custom production, materials, real product quality, and quote consultation.

### ProUniforma

Observed strengths:

- Strong SEO-style content.
- Covers multiple industries.
- Explains why uniforms matter.

Observed opportunity for Aksessoriuz:

- Uniforms should have their own landing page if the category remains strategically important.
- Tie uniforms to overall brand-experience production rather than treating them as isolated clothing.

### Alkograv

Position:

Observed strengths:

Observed opportunity for Aksessoriuz:

- Build stronger production-service pages with better visuals, clearer use cases, materials, and quote forms.

### POLIMAKS

URL:

- `https://polimax.uz/`

## Competitive Patterns

### What Competitors Do Well

- Some show prices, minimum quantities, and terms.
- Some lead with clear niche positioning.
- Premium packaging competitors use client logos and custom-project language.
- Print platforms use wide SEO coverage and product-level landing pages.
- Uniform competitors use long SEO content and industry-specific pages.

### Common Weaknesses

- Narrow positioning: many competitors own only one vertical.
- Weak UX consistency.
- Limited industry-based navigation.
- Little explanation of complete business outcomes.
- Template-like copy or inconsistent localization.
- Weak connection between products, materials, technologies, and business use cases.
- Quote flows are often basic and not tailored to product context.

## Opportunity For Aksessoriuz

Aksessoriuz can win by owning the middle ground between:

- a simple product catalog;
- a printing shop;
- a souvenir supplier;
- a premium packaging studio;
- a production-service workshop.

Recommended positioning:

Custom branded products, accessories, packaging, uniforms, and production services for businesses that need every physical detail to represent the brand professionally.

## Recommended Website Priorities

### Homepage Priority

1. Hero:
   - universal B2B production partner;
   - clear product scope;
   - quote-first CTA;
   - secondary catalog CTA.
2. Business-type entry:
   - restaurants and cafes;
   - hotels and hospitality;
   - corporate clients;
   - retail and packaging;
   - agencies/custom projects.
3. Product category groups:
   - restaurant accessories;
   - hotel accessories;
   - business products;
   - packaging and souvenirs;
   - uniforms and textile;
   - production services.
4. Real work/gallery:
   - show actual products and close-ups.
5. Materials and technologies:
   - eco-leather, natural leather, wood, acrylic, textile, paper, metal;
   - UV printing, laser engraving, embossing, foiling, printing.
6. Process:
   - choose product or send brief;
   - consult materials and branding;
   - approve sample/design;
   - produce and deliver.
7. Client proof:
   - logos, cases, industries served.
8. Quote and price-catalog block:
   - "Request exact calculation";
   - optional "View/download catalog with prices" when ready.

### Catalog Priority

The catalog should support three discovery modes:

1. By industry.
2. By product type.
3. By production technology/material.

Product cards should show:

- product name;
- category;
- short use case;
- materials;
- branding methods;
- optional MOQ;
- optional timeline;
- "Request calculation" CTA;
- "Order sample" CTA where relevant.

Avoid:

- generic "Buy" buttons;
- placeholder products;
- fixed prices unless maintained;
- forcing internal product codes as the main user-facing label.

### SEO Priority

Recommended SEO clusters:

1. Restaurant and cafe accessories:
   - menu covers in Tashkent;
   - menu folders;
   - check holders;
   - table tents;
   - reserves;
   - placemats;
   - napkin holders.
2. Hotel accessories:
   - hotel folders;
   - room guest folders;
   - branded hotel accessories;
   - key holders.
3. Corporate gifts and business products:
   - business sets with logo;
   - diaries with logo;
   - folders with logo;
   - cardholders;
   - corporate gifts in Tashkent.
4. Packaging:
   - branded packaging;
   - gift boxes;
   - packaging with logo;
   - premium packaging;
   - branded bags.
5. Production services:
   - UV printing in Tashkent;
   - laser cutting and engraving;
   - embossing logo;
   - foiling;
   - printing services.
6. Uniforms and textile:
   - restaurant uniforms;
   - branded uniforms;
   - aprons with logo;
   - textile products with logo.

Language note:

Initial structure should support Russian and English. Uzbek-language SEO should be considered after content resources and keyword validation.

## Recommended Priority Ranking

This ranking balances public market signals, competitor density, and Aksessoriuz catalog fit. It should be revisited once profitability and internal sales data are available.

1. HoReCa and hospitality accessories.
   - Strong market signal from accommodation and food-service volume.
   - Strong fit with existing product categories.
   - Recurring replacement and rebrand demand.
2. Packaging and branded presentation.
   - Strong retail/wholesale market signal.
   - Premium positioning opportunity.
   - Strong visual impact for website and cases.
3. Corporate gifts and business products.
   - Broad B2B base.
   - Strong fit with business sets, diaries, folders, cardholders.
   - Seasonal/event demand.
4. Production services.
   - Supports all product categories.
   - Good SEO entry point for users who know the technology they need.
5. Uniforms and textile.
   - Valuable but should be validated for profitability and operational capacity.
   - Needs its own page if actively promoted.

## Required Business Inputs Before Final IA

Need confirmation from the business:

- most profitable categories;
- average order value by category;
- repeat order frequency;
- minimum order quantities;
- production timelines;
- available photos by category;
- client logos and cases;
- whether price catalog will be public, downloadable, gated, or manager-sent;
