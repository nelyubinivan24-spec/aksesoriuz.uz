# Competitor and Reference Notes

## Purpose

Record competitive and reference observations for the website rebuild.

This initial version focuses on category-level observations from the current website audit and expected B2B buying behavior. A deeper external competitor review should be performed before final UX and SEO decisions.

## Current Website Weaknesses To Outperform

The new website must avoid these issues observed on the current public site:

- catalog structure is product-heavy but not buyer-journey oriented;
- many pages lack strong H1 and metadata structure;
- product cards do not consistently explain materials, use cases, branding options, or order process;
- some English pages contain Russian UI text;
- some contact details conflict across pages;
- placeholder/demo content appears in product pages;
- product actions use generic "Buy" language even when the real path is quote-based;
- sitemap and canonical structure need improvement;
- trust proof exists but is not organized into persuasive case-based storytelling.

## Competitive Standard To Aim For

The rebuilt website should feel closer to a premium B2B production partner than a local template catalog.

Important reference qualities:

- strong first-screen positioning;
- clear segmentation by customer type;
- high-quality product photography;
- fast route to quote request;
- visible proof of production capability;
- commercial product pages;
- mobile-first inquiry mechanics;
- structured SEO pages.

## Market Pattern: Common Weaknesses In Local B2B Sites

These are typical weaknesses to intentionally avoid:

- unclear offer on the homepage;
- catalog without strategy;
- weak mobile CTA;
- too many categories with no guidance;
- product photos without context;
- no explanation of production process;
- no answer to minimum order, timing, or material questions;
- contact forms with too many or too few fields;
- weak SEO metadata;
- generic corporate language.

## Strategic Advantage Opportunity

Aksessoriuz can differentiate by combining:

- product catalog;
- industry-specific pages;
- production capabilities;
- material education;
- quote mechanics;
- case studies;
- real client proof.

This combination can make the website more useful than a standard catalog and more credible than a generic manufacturing landing page.

## Reference UX Patterns To Use

Recommended patterns:

- industry entry cards on homepage;
- "choose your business type" navigation;
- product cards with material, branding method, and use-case tags;
- quote request drawer or modal;
- mobile sticky contact bar;
- product inquiry with preselected product name;
- production process section;
- material and branding method explainers;
- case cards grouped by industry.

## Reference SEO Patterns To Use

Recommended patterns:

- unique title and description per page;
- canonical URLs;
- language alternates;
- product/category schema where relevant;
- organization schema;
- local business schema;
- optimized category landing copy;
- FAQ sections on commercial pages;
- image alt text based on product and material.

## External Competitor Review Backlog

Before finalizing SEO and page hierarchy, perform market and competitor research. Audience priority should not be based on assumptions alone.

Research should help determine:

- which B2B segments show strongest demand;
- which categories appear most commercially competitive;
- which search queries are likely to bring qualified leads;
- how local and regional competitors structure catalogs and quote flows;
- where Aksessoriuz can differentiate.

Review:

- local Uzbekistan producers of menu covers and branded business accessories;
- regional restaurant accessory suppliers;
- promotional gift and corporate merchandise websites;
- packaging production websites;
- international B2B manufacturing websites with strong quote flows.

For each competitor/reference, capture:

- positioning;
- catalog structure;
- CTA pattern;
- product card quality;
- trust proof;
- SEO structure;
- mobile UX;
- gaps Aksessoriuz can exploit.
