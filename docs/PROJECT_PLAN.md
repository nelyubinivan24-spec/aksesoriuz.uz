# Project Master Plan

## Objective

Build a new high-converting B2B website for Aksessoriuz. The new site should keep the business essence of the current website while replacing the design, structure, UX mechanics, content system, and codebase.

Primary business goal: maximize qualified client requests from restaurants, hotels, corporate clients, retailers, and companies that need branded products, packaging, uniforms, and production services.

## Success Criteria

- Visitors understand the company offer within the first screen.
- Each key audience has a clear path to relevant products and services.
- Catalog pages guide users toward inquiry, sample request, or direct contact.
- Product cards clearly answer: what it is, who it is for, materials, branding options, minimum order logic, and how to request pricing.
- Website is fast, mobile-first, SEO-ready, and technically maintainable.
- Analytics can measure lead actions.
