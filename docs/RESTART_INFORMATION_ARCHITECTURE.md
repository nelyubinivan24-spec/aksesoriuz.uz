# Restart Information Architecture

Date: 2026-05-31

## Goal

Create a professional B2B website structure that preserves the full original Aksessoriuz commercial scope while making the buying journey clearer, more premium, and more conversion-focused.

The website must help a visitor answer:

- What can Aksessoriuz produce?
- Is this relevant for my business?
- Can they make it with my logo, material, quantity, and deadline?
- Do they look trustworthy enough to contact?
- What should I send to get a quote?

## Homepage Structure

### 1. Hero

Purpose:

- immediately explain custom branded production for business;
- show real product imagery in the first viewport;
- keep the transparent/dark product-led background direction the client liked;
- avoid abstract decoration.

Required content:

- H1: branded production for restaurants, hotels, companies, retail, and events;
- short paragraph about custom production with logo, materials, quantity, and deadlines;
- CTA: request calculation;
- CTA: view products;
- trust strip: 12+ years, 1,500+ clients, since 2013, custom B2B production.

### 2. Product Directions

Show the main product directions with strong images:

- restaurant and cafe accessories;
- hotel accessories;
- business products and corporate gifts;
- packaging and souvenirs;
- uniforms and textile;
- production services.

Each card must show:

- category name;
- examples of products;
- best-fit buyers;
- CTA to category.

### 3. Choose By Business Task

Buyer-oriented paths:

- open a restaurant or update service accessories;
- prepare hotel guest-facing materials;
- create corporate gifts or branded office products;
- produce premium packaging;
- dress a team in branded uniform;
- brand existing products with printing, engraving, embossing, or foiling.

### 4. Production Capabilities

Show technologies as a serious production capability:

- UV printing;
- laser cutting and engraving;
- embossing;
- foiling;
- printing / polygraphy;
- marking;
- carpentry;
- textile branding;
- food photography if confirmed.

### 5. Materials And Finishing

Explain product quality through materials:

- eco-leather;
- natural leather where applicable;
- wood;
- plywood;
- acrylic;
- metal;
- paper/cardboard;
- textile;
- mixed materials.

### 6. Work / Proof

Show real clients and finished product examples:

- brand logos;
- product photos;
- short case snippets;
- industries served;
- proof points from original company page.

### 7. Process

Make the B2B buying cycle clear:

1. client sends product idea, reference, quantity, logo, deadline;
2. manager helps choose material, construction, and branding;
3. quote and sample/details are agreed;
4. layout is approved;
5. batch is produced and delivered.

### 8. Lead Block

Short project brief:

- product interest;
- quantity;
- material / branding method;
- logo or file upload later;
- deadline;
- name, phone, Telegram/WhatsApp.

## Category Page Blueprint

Each category page must include:

1. Hero with real product image and commercial positioning.
2. Subcategory selector.
3. Product/reference grid with meaningful cards.
4. Material and branding method explanation.
5. Best-fit industries.
6. Production process for this category.
7. Related technologies.
8. FAQ.
9. Quote CTA.

The page must not show migration counts or internal source terminology.

## Product Card Blueprint

A product card should include:

- product image;
- product name;
- SKU/article;
- product family;
- compact use case;
- material tags;
- branding method tags;
- optional "popular for" label;
- CTA: Рассчитать тираж;
- secondary CTA: Подробнее.

Avoid:

- "buy";
- "add to cart";
- "draft";
- "example item" as the main value;
- repeated generic descriptions.

## Product Detail Page Blueprint

Sections:

1. Image gallery and product summary.
2. Commercial description.
3. Suitable for.
4. Materials and finish options.
5. Branding methods.
6. Possible sizes / construction notes, if available.
7. Quantity and timing explanation without fake numbers.
8. Similar products.
9. Related production services.
10. Quote form with this product preselected.

## Industries Section

Industry pages:

- restaurants and cafes;
- hotels and hospitality;
- corporate clients;
- retail and product brands;
- events and agencies;
- government and institutions if strategically useful.

Each industry page:

- starts with buyer need;
- shows relevant product bundles;
- shows proof and process;
- leads to quote.

## Production Section

Production is not a generic services list. It should prove capability.

Each service page must explain:

- what the technology does;
- which materials it works with;
- which products use it;
- visual examples;
- quality constraints and why manager consultation matters;
- CTA to discuss production.

## Work Section

Current "work" pages should become case/proof pages.

Minimum structure:

- client / industry;
- product type;
- task;
- materials;
- branding method;
- result;
- gallery.

If final case details are not available, use curated product galleries and client logo proof, but do not invent case claims.

## Contact Section

Contact page must be a conversion page, not a generic footer repeat.

Required:

- phone;
- Telegram/WhatsApp if confirmed;
- address;
- working hours if confirmed;
- short quote form;
- map;
- what to prepare before contacting: product type, quantity, logo, deadline, material preferences.

## SEO And Redirect Strategy

SEO must preserve the original site's coverage.

Required:

- map all original category URLs to new category pages;
- map all original product URLs to product pages, family pages, or redirects;
- keep canonical URLs stable;
- add localized RU/EN alternate links;
- write SEO titles/descriptions for categories, services, and product families;
- avoid creating thin pages for all 255 products until content is useful.

## Acceptance Criteria

The restarted structure is accepted only when:

- every original product group has a new public destination;
- every original service has a new public destination;
- every original product URL has a redirect or content decision;
- no public page exposes internal project language;
- the catalog works as a buyer tool, not as a migration table;
- every core page has a quote-first conversion path;
- desktop and mobile layouts are checked visually;
- language switching keeps users in the expected language.
